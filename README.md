# Privacy Policy for Tribal Jeevan Saathi

**Last updated: October 2026**

Tribal Jeevan Saathi ("we", "our", or "us") provides a matchmaking platform tailored for tribal communities across India. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Profile Information:** Name, age, gender, community/tribe, religion, qualification, occupation, district, state, and bio.
- **Photos:** Photos uploaded voluntarily for profile verification and matchmaking.
- **Account Credentials:** Login details used to secure your account.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
- To create and manage your matchmaking profile.
- To recommend relevant matches based on community, district, and preferences.
- To enable communication between matched members.

### 3. Data Protection & Security
We use industry-standard measures to safeguard your personal data. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third-party advertisers.

### 4. User Rights & Account Deletion
You can edit or delete your profile information anytime directly from the app or request full deletion of your account and personal records by contacting support.

### 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or support requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the app support section.
